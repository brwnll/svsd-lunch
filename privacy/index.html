SVSD Lunch
School lunch menus & reminders

Privacy Policy

Effective November 16, 2026

SVSD Lunch doesn't collect, store or share your personal information.

No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking

The app has no sign-in, and it doesn't measure how you use it or show ads.

Your settings stay on your device

The school you choose, the days you mark to buy lunch, your picks and your reminder times are saved only on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

Menus

To show menus, the app downloads the Snoqualmie Valley School District's published lunch menus from the district's menu provider, Nutrislice. Those requests include which school's menu to send and, like any internet request, your device's IP address. They include nothing that identifies you. Nutrislice's privacy policy covers how it handles requests.

Siri, Spotlight and notifications

Requests you make to Siri are handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. The app tells iOS which actions you use, and adds upcoming menus to Spotlight, so iOS can suggest them; iOS keeps this on your device. Reminders are scheduled on your device.

Children

The app doesn't collect information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new effective date.
