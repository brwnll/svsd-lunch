SVSD Lunch shows school lunch menus for Snoqualmie Valley School District schools, reminds you on the days your kid buys lunch, and answers Siri.

Siri doesn't answer

Open the app once after installing it, then say "What's School Lunch today?". Every phrase needs the words "School Lunch". The phrases are listed under Ask Siri in the app.

Wrong school

Tap the school's name under the title to choose another.

A day says the menu isn't posted

The district hasn't published that week yet. The app picks it up as soon as it's posted.

Reminders don't appear

Check Settings > Notifications > SVSD Lunch. Reminder times are set with the bell button in the app.

Something else, or a menu looks wrong? Let us know (needs a free GitHub account).

For prices, allergens or meal accounts, contact your school.
